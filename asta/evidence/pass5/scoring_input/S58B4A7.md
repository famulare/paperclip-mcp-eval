## Prompt 1 — Central mechanistic claim

The central empirical claim is that adding the mucosal adjuvant dmLT to intramuscular IPV did NOT improve intestinal (mucosal) or humoral immunity, nor reduce fecal poliovirus shedding after oral polio challenge. The abstract's Conclusions state: "Addition of dmLT to IPV administered intramuscularly neither affected humoral nor intestinal immunity nor decreased fecal virus shedding following bOPV challenge." The underlying mechanistic motivation is stated in Background: "Inactivated trivalent poliovirus vaccine (IPV) induces humoral immunity, which protects against paralytic poliomyelitis but does not induce sufficient mucosal immunity to block intestinal infection," and the study "assessed the intestinal immunity in healthy adults in Belgium conferred by a co-formulation of IPV with the mucosal adjuvant double mutant Labile Toxin (dmLT) derived from Escherichia coli." The Discussion excerpts reinforces the null result: "we failed to observe any impact of dmLT on the intestinal or humoral immunogenicity of co-administered IPV."

## Prompt 2 — Evidence supporting it

The abstract and Discussion excerpts supply the supporting evidence:

- **Design/randomization (Methods, abstract):** "Healthy fully IPV-vaccinated 18–45-year-olds were randomly allocated to three groups: on Day 1 two groups received one full dose of IPV (n = 30) or IPV + dmLT (n = 30) in a blinded manner, and the third received an open-label dose of bivalent live oral polio vaccine (bOPV types 1 and 3, n = 20). All groups received a challenge dose of bOPV on Day 29."
- **Outcome measurement (Methods, abstract):** "Mucosal immune responses were measured by fecal neutralization and IgA on Days 29 and 43, with fecal shedding of challenge viruses measured for 28 days. Humoral responses were measured by serum neutralizing antibody (NAb)."
- **Shedding result (abstract):** "Fecal shedding of challenge viruses in both IPV groups exceeded that of the bOPV group but was not different between IPV and IPV + dmLT groups."
- **Humoral result (abstract):** "High serum NAb responses were observed in both IPV groups, alongside modest levels of fecal neutralization and IgA."
- **Discussion detail:** "both IPV and bOPV induced high levels of humoral neutralizing antibodies and seroconversion for all three poliovirus types (with the obvious exception of type 2 for bOPV). Neither humoral nor intestinal immunogenicity were increased by dmLT; indeed, the magnitude of humoral responses measured as geometric mean-fold rises were generally lower after IPV + dmLT than IPV alone."
- **Shedding by serotype (Discussion):** "Fecal viral shedding was generally higher in IPV-treated participants for poliovirus type 3 compared with type 1."
- **Safety evidence (Discussion):** "There were no SAEs, deaths, or withdrawals due to an AE reported and only one related adverse event was considered to be severe -a participant in the IPV + dmLT group displayed a transient elevation of aspartate aminotransferase (AST) level which resolved spontaneously within 10 days."

## Prompt 4 — Expert eye

Several points a domain expert would flag are supported by the bundle:

- **Population is high-baseline-immune adults, not the target population.** The Discussion explicitly limits generalizability: "the different age and routes of administration -adults from a high-income country given intramuscular dmLT in the present study rather than 6-59month-old children from a LMIC who received dmLT with oral ETEC vaccine [15], as well as the high baseline seropositivity of participants in this study may have limited any measurable adjuvanting effect of the dmLT."
- **Single dose tested.** "In this study we only selected and evaluated one dose of dmLT which was generally well tolerated by the vaccinees" — a null result at one dose does not exclude activity at others; the authors note tolerability "may suggest the utility of another clinical assessment with higher doses of dmLT."
- **Route matters.** dmLT was given intramuscularly here, whereas its preclinical/clinical adjuvant activity is cited for "intramuscular or intradermal routes" and "oral ETEC vaccine"; the mucosal-adjuvant rationale (blocking intestinal infection) may be route-sensitive.
- **Direction of the humoral point estimate.** An expert would note the humoral response was not merely unchanged but numerically lower with adjuvant: "the magnitude of humoral responses measured as geometric mean-fold rises were generally lower after IPV + dmLT than IPV alone."
- **bOPV type-2 caveat.** Seroconversion occurred for all three types "with the obvious exception of type 2 for bOPV" — expected since bOPV contains only types 1 and 3.

## Prompt 5 — Overclaim risk

- **Do not generalize the null to children/LMIC or to other doses/routes.** The authors themselves bound the claim: baseline seropositivity, adult high-income population, single intramuscular dose "may have limited any measurable adjuvanting effect," and "the current data do not support IPV and dmLT as a solution to improving the intestinal immune response to IPV" — a conditional, not a universal, negative.
- **Do not read this as evidence dmLT is inert as an adjuvant generally.** The bundle notes dmLT "has been shown to have potent adjuvant capacity in preclinical animal models ... and in early clinical studies," so the null is specific to this co-formulation/route/population.
- **Do not overclaim safety beyond phase 1 scope.** Safety statements ("no SAEs, deaths, or withdrawals due to an AE") come from a small phase-1 study (n = 30/30/20 per the abstract); rare-event safety cannot be claimed.
- **Surrogate-marker interpretation is tentative.** The a4b7 gut-homing ASC assessment is framed as exploratory: prior work only "hypothesized" these cells "could serve as a surrogate marker," and "Further studies were recommended ... to solidify any conclusions on the relevance of these cells."
